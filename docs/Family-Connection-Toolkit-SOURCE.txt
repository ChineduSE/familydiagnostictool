Part 1: The 12 diagnostic questions
Each question uses a 1–5 scale: 1 = Never, 2 = Rarely, 3 = Sometimes, 4 = Often, 5 = Always. Maximum score: 60 points.

Section A: Communication patterns

Q1  Communication patterns
When your child comes to you upset or frustrated, do you stop what you’re doing to listen — without immediately offering advice or solutions?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Q2  Communication patterns
In a typical week, how often do you and your child have a real conversation — not about schedules, homework, or chores, but about how they’re actually feeling?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Section B: Emotional availability

Q3  Emotional availability
When your child is having a hard day, can they show you they’re struggling — without worrying about your reaction or being told to “toughen up”?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Q4  Emotional availability
After a difficult moment between you and your child — an argument, a punishment, a misunderstanding — do you find a way to reconnect before the day ends?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Section C: Device habits

Q5  Device habits
During family meals or dedicated family time, are screens — yours and your child’s — put away without negotiation?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Q6  Device habits
When your child reaches for a screen, do they usually do so because they’re genuinely bored or seeking entertainment — rather than avoiding you or an uncomfortable feeling?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Section D: Family routines

Q7  Family routines
Does your family have at least one shared routine each day — a meal, a bedtime ritual, a morning check-in — that feels like genuine togetherness rather than just logistics?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Q8  Family routines
When life gets busy or stressful, does your family maintain the habits and rituals that keep you close — or do they quietly disappear?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Section E: Parent-child bonding

Q9  Parent-child bonding
Does your child seek you out — not because they need something, but simply because they want to be near you or share something with you?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Q10  Parent-child bonding
Do you and your child share at least one activity — a hobby, a show, a walk, a joke — that belongs just to the two of you?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Section F: Behavior triggers

Q11  Behavior triggers
When your child acts out, pushes back, or shuts down, do you find yourself able to pause and ask “what’s behind this?” before reacting?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always

Q12  Behavior triggers
After setting a boundary or consequence, does your child understand why — and do they still feel loved by you?
Scale:  1 = Never     2 = Rarely     3 = Sometimes     4 = Often     5 = Always
